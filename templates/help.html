Help & How-To Guides

This app is designed to help you keep track of everything you need to do, so you can stop using your brain as a storage device. The main idea is simple:

  1. Capture Everything: Use the Inbox (or the "Add Task" button) to get thoughts and to-dos out of your head and into the system. Don't worry about organizing them yet, just get them in.
  2. Organize Your Tasks: From the Inbox, turn your thoughts into clear actions. Give them a name, and if they are part of a bigger goal, add them to a Project.
  3. Work from the Board: The Board is your main workspace. It shows you what's ready to be worked on, what you're currently doing, and what's done. Just drag tasks from left to right as you make progress!

It's a simple but important difference:

  • A Task is a single, physical action. "Call the plumber," "Buy milk," or "Draft the email."
  • A Project is any goal that requires more than one task to complete. "Renovate the Kitchen" is a project. The tasks would be "Hire a contractor," "Choose new cabinets," "Buy appliances," etc.

If you have a big goal, create a Project for it. Then, add all the small steps as tasks within that project. This keeps your board clean and helps you focus on one step at a time.

The Lists section is perfect for simple checklists that aren't part of a big project. It's great for things like:

  • Grocery shopping lists
  • Packing lists for a trip
  • A list of movies to watch

You can create as many lists as you want. The "General Shopping List" is a special list that acts as a catch-all for quick items you need to buy.

The Icebox is a holding area for tasks that you've processed from your inbox, but aren't ready to actively work on yet.

Think of it as your "on deck" list. The tasks are defined and ready to go, but keeping them in the Icebox prevents your main Board from getting cluttered. When you're ready to start on a new batch of work, you can move items from the Icebox to the 'Ready' column.

This keeps your active board clean and focused only on what you've committed to doing soon.

The Someday/Maybe list is for things you might want to do, but not right now. It's a "low-pressure" list for incubating ideas.

Good candidates for this list are:

  • Trips you want to take someday (e.g., "Learn to scuba dive").
  • Books to read or movies to watch.
  • Projects you're not committed to yet (e.g., "Build a deck").

Putting these items here keeps them out of your active system, but ensures you won't forget about them. You can review this list periodically and activate items when the time is right.

The Weekly Review is arguably the most critical habit for making GTD work. It's the time you set aside each week to keep your system clean, current, and trusted.

The Review Page helps you with the three main steps:

  1. Get Clear: Process everything in your Inbox to zero.
  2. Get Current: Review your active projects and "next actions" to make sure they are still relevant. Check on items you're waiting for.
  3. Get Creative: Look over your Someday/Maybe list for any ideas you want to activate.

Doing this weekly ensures nothing falls through the cracks and you can trust that your system is a complete picture of your world.
